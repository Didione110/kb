## 问题现象

## 问题原因

一笔出库单开具多张发票，需要在出库单查询里面显示所有发票号码，不能重复显示单据

## 解决方案

行转列语句：
oracle
select billno,listagg(logisticcode,',') within group(order by logisticcode) as logisticcode
 ,listagg(shippercode,',') within group(order by shippercode) as shippercode from k_wms_sendlog group by billno
 
sql
(select invoicecode+'/' from (select a.entid,invoicecode,rfbillno from invoicemt a join invoicedt b on a.billno=b.billno 
 and a.entid=b.entid group by a.entid,invoicecode,rfbillno) y
 where a.billno=y.rfbillno and a.entid=y.entid for XML PATH('')) as invoicecode
