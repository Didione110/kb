## 问题原因

可以做成返回调度功能，各种情况下不能取消的注意要添加拦截

## 解决方案

begin 
delete wmspickmt where billno =:billno and entid=:_entid;

delete wmspickdt where billno =:billno and entid=:_entid;

delete wmspickareadt where billno =:billno and entid=:_entid;

end;
