## 问题原因

关于采购单对应多单采购入库单的情况，如何关联查询。针对多次入库的情况，查询对应采购入库单

## 解决方案

select a.billcode,DBMS_LOB.SUBSTR(wm_concat(distinct d.billcode ))
From purnotesmt a inner join purnotesdt b on a.billno=b.billno
inner join purindt c on c.rfbillno=b.billno and c.rfbillsn=b.billsn
inner join purinmt d on c.billno=d.billno
where a.dates&gt;='2024-10-18' and a.billcode like 'PKP%'
group by a.billcode
