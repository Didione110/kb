## 问题现象

打印问题：多组织，单据打印的时候，自动获取现存量

## 问题原因

现存量：select sum(UFIDA::U9::InvTrans::WhQoh::WhQoh.StoreQty) as 库存在手量_数量 from UFIDA::U9::InvTrans::WhQoh::WhQoh where UFIDA::U9::InvTrans::WhQoh::WhQoh.ItemInfo.ItemCode=(select top 1 UFIDA::U9::PM::Rcv::Receivement.RcvLines.ItemInfo.ItemCode as 料品_料号 from UFIDA::U9::PM::Rcv::Receivement where UFIDA::U9::PM::Rcv::Receivement.Org.ID=Contexts!OrgID.Value and UFIDA::U9::PM::Rcv::Receivement.RcvLines.DocLineNo=Fields!Receivement_RcvLines_DocLineNo.Value and UFIDA::U9::PM::Rcv::Receivement.DocNo=Fields!Receivement_DocNo.Value )

## 解决方案

and 组织条件 ：UFIDA::U9::InvTrans::WhQoh::WhQoh.LogisticOrg.ID=Contexts!OrgID.Value
