## 解决方案

UBF OQL语句如下：
select UFIDA::U9::PM::PO::PurchaseOrder.POLines.SrcDocInfo.SrcDocNo as 来源单据_来源单据号 from UFIDA::U9::PM::PO::PurchaseOrder where UFIDA::U9::PM::PO::PurchaseOrder.Org.ID=U_DF.GetQLValue(Contexts!OrgID.Value) and UFIDA::U9::PM::PO::PurchaseOrder.POLines.SrcDocInfo.SrcDocLineNo=U_DF.GetQLValue(Fields!SrcDocInfo_SrcDocLineNo.Value) and UFIDA::U9::PM::PO::PurchaseOrder.POLines.SrcDocInfo.SrcDocNo=U_DF.GetQLValue(Fields!SrcDocInfo_SrcDocNo.Value)
