## 问题原因

## 解决方案

delete from iufo_userinfo where user_code = '15072363686' and unit_id = (select unit_id from iufo_unit_info where unit_code = '010202');删除时候提示下面两张表有数据，可以执行对应语句删除对应表数据delete from iufo_user_org where pk_user = (select user_id from iufo_userinfo where user_code = '15072363686' and unit_id = (select unit_id from iufo_unit_info where unit_code = '010202')); delete from iufo_user_role where user_id = (select user_id from iufo_userinfo where user_code = '15072363686' and unit_id = (select unit_id from iufo_unit_info where unit_code = '010202'));
