## 问题现象

调用移动审批接口后,u9c单据状态未变更

## 问题原因

1：u9c移动审批流未配置好
2：获取token时添加的审批人编码错误

## 解决方案

select E.Code as UserCode
 from ${tableName} A
 inner join CS_Workflow_FlowInstance B on A.FlowInstance=b.ID
 inner join CS_Workflow_FlowInstance_Trl B1 ON b.id=B1.ID and B1.SysMLFlag='zh-CN'
 inner join CS_Workflow_FlowState C on C.ID=B.CurrentFlowState
 inner join CS_Workflow_WaitingUser D on C.ID=D.FlowState
 left join Base_User E on E.ID=d.UID
 where A.docno = #{docno}
使用语句查询对应单据审批人编码
