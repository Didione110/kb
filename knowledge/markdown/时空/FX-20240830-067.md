## 问题现象

## 问题原因

查询所有工作流对应审批结点对应角色

## 解决方案

select distinct wfcode,wfname,b.taskname,d.rolecode,d.rolename 
from WfSchemaUser a inner join WfWork b on a.wfid=b.wfid
inner join WfUserDesc c on b.taskid=c.taskid
inner join roledoc d on c.executant=d.roleid
order by 1,3,4
