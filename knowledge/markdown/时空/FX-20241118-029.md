## 问题现象

DHERP-WMS分两个企业，销售出库复核记录缺失

## 问题原因

没有存储关键表gspoutmt dt 表中

## 解决方案

取WMS 三张表的数据进行汇总
select * 
from WmsCompMt a 
 join WmsCompDt b on a.billno = b.billno and a.entid =b.entid 
 join wmsoutdt c on b.rfbillno =c.billno and b.rfbillsn =c.billsn and b.entid =c.entid 
 join wmsoutmt d on c.billno =d.billno and c.entid =d.entid 
 left join wmsunpackdt cc on c.billno = cc.rfbillno and c.billsn =cc.rfbillsn and c.entid =cc.entd
 left join wmsunpackmt dd on cc.billno =dd.billno and cc.entid =dd.entid
 left join staffdoc p on d.fhyid =p.staffid and d.entid =p.entid 
上述表对应是订单表wmsoutmt dt，整件和零散的业务表

复核人员取不同表中数据
case when nvl(d.fhyid,' ') =' ' then m.staffname 
else p.staffname end as Acceptname,
