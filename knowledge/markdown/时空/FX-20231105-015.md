## 解决方案

--查看那个表死锁
select object_name(resource_associated_entity_id) as tableName, request_session_id as pid 
from sys.dm_tran_locks
where resource_type = 'OBJECT' --and object_name(resource_associated_entity_id)='spkfk'

--杀掉进程pid
KILL 83--pid

通过查看对应锁表内容，对应进程无释放的情况下，可以强行关闭对应进程后，加入字段
