## 问题现象

提取上游码上放心出库数据时，需要存储最小码

## 问题原因

常规方案需要拼接多段SQL，开销比较大，使用Oracle递归查询方案

## 解决方案

使用Oracle递归查询方案，会递归查询大转中，中转小。示例：
with jgm (entid,orgid,rfbillno,rfbillcode,rfbillsn
 ,goodsid,batchcode,IMEINO,rfid,rfname)
as (
 select a.entid,a.orgid
 ,a.billno as rfbillno,a.billcode as rfbillcode,b.billsn as rfbillsn
 ,b.goodsid,b.batchcode
 ,to_char(f.ecode) as IMEINO
 ,j.orgid as rfid,j.orgname as rfname
 from gspinmt a
 join gspindt b on a.billno=b.billno and a.entid=b.entid
 join joinpurdt c on b.rfbillno=c.billno and b.rfbillsn=c.billsn and b.entid=c.entid
 join joinoutdt d on c.rebillno=d.billno and c.rebillsn=d.billsn and c.entid=d.entid
 join kq_hbcbwms_outact_b e on d.rfbillcode=to_char(e.docid)||'.0' and d.rfbillsn=e.lno and b.batchcode=e.lotno
 join kq_hbcbwms_zsm f on e.wltxm=f.erporderno and e.lno=f.erpordernodtl and e.lotno=f.lotno
 join orgdoc j on a.supplyid=j.orgid and a.entid=j.entid
 where a.entid='E3ACDA7KPUA' and a.billno = 11217483 
 UNION ALL
 SELECT a.entid,a.orgid
 ,a.rfbillno,a.rfbillcode,a.rfbillsn
 ,a.goodsid,a.batchcode
 ,to_char(g.code) as IMEINO
 ,a.rfid,a.rfname
 FROM JGM a
 join k_lsyd_drugcode g on a.IMEINO=g.parent_code 
)

select * from jgm ;
